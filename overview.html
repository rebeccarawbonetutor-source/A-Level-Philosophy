Foundations & Comparison

Mental States & Frameworks

  • Foundations: Incorporates conscious and unconscious states, intentionality, phenomenal properties (qualia), and introspective access.
  • Substance Dualism: Minds exist and are distinct from bodies (Mind = thinking/unextended; Body = extended/unthinking). Replaced historically by frameworks like Plato's dualism (immortal soul vs mortal body using the Argument from Recollection).
  • Property Dualism: Physical substances have irreducibly non-physical mental properties.
  • Physicalism: Rejects non-physical substances.
